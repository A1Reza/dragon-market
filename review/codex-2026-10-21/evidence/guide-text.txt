Launch the interactive workbench

5. Business invariant correctness — expert code review
CRITICAL RULE  The key rule selected by the project is: S(d) + R <= L, where S(d) is completed spending on the current UTC day, R is all outstanding holds regardless of age, and L is the positive daily limit. A zero limit means unlimited. The accounting rule is a specification to critically assess, not something reviewers must automatically agree with.

Also verify: total gold changes only for actual debits/earnings; available gold = gold − reserved; reserves and spend require adequate capacity; settlement converts an existing hold to spending without double-counting.
